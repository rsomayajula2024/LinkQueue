## Name
Connect Later

## Summary (max 132 chars)
Hit your weekly invite limit? Save profiles to invite later, get a reminder when it resets, and work through them fast.

## Category
Productivity

## Description
Ran out of weekly connection invites just as you found someone worth connecting with? Connect Later remembers them for you.

HOW IT WORKS
• Click "+ Connect Later" on any profile to add it to your queue
• Log the day you hit your weekly limit, and get a notification when it resets
• Open the dashboard, tick the people you want, and click Start
• Each profile opens in turn. Click "Prepare invite" to open the invite dialog with your note filled in, then click Send. The next profile opens automatically.

FEATURES
• Default invite note with {firstName} and {myName}, or a custom note per person
• Checkbox queue with select all
• Sent list with CSV export
• Skips profiles where an invite is already pending
• Pauses automatically if you hit the limit mid-session

SAFE BY DESIGN
Connect Later never sends invites on its own. You click Send on every invite, so your account stays within the site's rules.

PRIVATE
Everything stays in your browser. No accounts, no servers, no tracking, no ads. Free and open source.

Connect Later is an independent tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by LinkedIn.

## Single purpose (privacy tab)
Lets users save professional profiles to send connection invites to later, and reminds them when their weekly invite limit resets.

## Permission justifications (privacy tab)
- storage: Saves the user's queue of profiles, notes and settings locally in the browser.
- alarms: Periodically checks whether the user's weekly invite limit has reset so the extension can remind them.
- notifications: Notifies the user when their weekly invite limit has reset and profiles are waiting.
- Host permission (linkedin.com): Adds the "Connect Later" button to profile pages and helps open the invite dialog when the user clicks "Prepare invite".

## Remote code
No, the extension does not use remote code.
